# Northfield University Scholarship Policy (Synthetic Demonstration Document)

> This is a fictional policy document written for the ScholarAI Workforce demo.
> It does not represent any real university's actual scholarship rules.

## 1. Overview

This policy governs the award of undergraduate scholarships at the University
of Northfield. Three scholarship programs are covered: the Academic Merit
Scholarship, the Financial Need-Based Scholarship, and the Leadership &
Community Impact Scholarship. Every award decision must be reviewed and
approved by a human member of the Scholarship Committee; no scholarship is
awarded automatically.

## 2. Academic Merit Scholarship

### 2.1 Purpose

Awarded primarily on sustained academic excellence across a student's
enrollment at the university.

### 2.2 Eligibility Requirements

A student is eligible for the Academic Merit Scholarship if they meet all of
the following:

- Minimum cumulative CGPA of 3.50 on a 4.00 scale.
- At least 30 credits completed.
- Currently enrolled in semester 2 or later.
- No failed courses in the two most recent semesters.
- A current academic transcript on file with the Registrar.

### 2.3 Required Documents

- Official academic transcript.

### 2.4 Priority Criteria

Preference is given to students demonstrating an improving academic trend
across semesters, and to students with documented extracurricular leadership
or research achievement, in addition to a high CGPA.

## 3. Financial Need-Based Scholarship

### 3.1 Purpose

Awarded primarily on demonstrated financial need, with a baseline academic
standing requirement so that the award still supports continued enrollment.

### 3.2 Eligibility Requirements

- Minimum cumulative CGPA of 2.50 on a 4.00 scale.
- At least 15 credits completed.
- Currently enrolled in semester 1 or later.
- No more than 2 failed courses total.
- A completed Financial Aid Application Form on file.

### 3.3 Required Documents

- Official academic transcript.
- Financial Aid Application Form (family income, household size, dependents,
  tuition cost, and any financial aid already received).

### 3.4 Priority Criteria

Priority is given to households with an annual tuition-to-income ratio above
0.5, and to households supporting three or more dependents.

## 4. Leadership & Community Impact Scholarship

### 4.1 Purpose

Awarded for leadership, volunteering, and community contribution alongside a
solid (not necessarily top) academic record.

### 4.2 Eligibility Requirements

- Minimum cumulative CGPA of 3.00 on a 4.00 scale.
- At least 30 credits completed.
- Currently enrolled in semester 2 or later.
- No more than 1 failed course total.
- A letter of recommendation from a faculty member or club/organization
  advisor confirming the applicant's leadership role.

### 4.3 Required Documents

- Official academic transcript.
- Recommendation letter.

### 4.4 Priority Criteria

Preference is given to applicants who have held a leadership position
(club president, team captain, project lead) for at least one full academic
year, or who can document sustained (not one-off) volunteering.

## 5. Disciplinary Conditions

An applicant with an active disciplinary sanction on file at the time of
application is not eligible for any scholarship listed in this policy until
the sanction has been resolved. Disciplinary status is verified by the
Registrar's Office and is outside the scope of the automated evaluation
system; the Eligibility Agent flags disciplinary review as a required
document only when the applicant's record indicates one is needed.

## 6. Human Review Requirement

No scholarship award is final until a designated human reviewer has approved
the recommendation produced by the evaluation workflow. The reviewer may
approve, reject, request further review, or request additional information
from the applicant. The reviewer's decision, and their reasoning, must be
recorded against the application record.
