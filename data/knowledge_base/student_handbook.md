# Northfield University Student Handbook - Scholarships Excerpt (Synthetic Demonstration Document)

> Fictional document written for the ScholarAI Workforce demo.

## 1. How to Apply

Students apply for a scholarship by submitting their supporting documents
(transcript, and, where required by the specific scholarship, a financial
aid application form or a recommendation letter) through the university's
scholarship portal. Applications are reviewed by the ScholarAI Workforce
evaluation system and then by a human member of the Scholarship Committee.

## 2. What to Expect

1. The system reads and extracts information from the submitted documents.
2. Specialist review agents check eligibility, academic performance,
   financial need, and achievements, and retrieve the relevant scholarship
   policy sections.
3. A verification step cross-checks the submitted information for
   consistency.
4. An overall recommendation is produced with full supporting evidence.
5. A human reviewer makes the final decision: approve, reject, request
   review, or request more information from the applicant.

## 3. Appeals

A student may request a review of a scholarship decision within 14 days of
notification by contacting the Scholarship Committee directly. Appeals are
handled by a human reviewer and are outside the scope of the automated
evaluation system.

## 4. Academic Integrity

Any document found to contain falsified information (e.g. an altered
transcript) will result in immediate disqualification from all university
scholarships and referral to the Academic Integrity Office.
