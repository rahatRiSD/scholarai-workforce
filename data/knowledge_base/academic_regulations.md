# Northfield University Academic Regulations (Synthetic Demonstration Document)

> Fictional document written for the ScholarAI Workforce demo.

## 1. Grading and CGPA

Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is computed on a 4.00 scale across all
completed, non-withdrawn courses. A grade of F in any course counts as a
failed course for scholarship eligibility purposes, regardless of whether the
course was later retaken and passed.

## 2. Academic Standing

### 2.1 Good Standing

A student is in good academic standing if their CGPA is at or above 2.00 and
they have no more than one failed course in their most recent two semesters.

### 2.2 Academic Probation

A student with a CGPA below 2.00 for two consecutive semesters is placed on
academic probation and is not eligible for any university scholarship until
good standing is restored.

## 3. Enrollment and Semester Progression

Students are expected to complete at least 12 credits per semester to be
considered full-time. Scholarship eligibility calculations that reference
"current semester" use the student's officially registered semester number
as recorded by the Registrar, not the number of terms since first
enrollment (which can differ for students who took an approved leave).

## 4. Transcript Requests

Official transcripts are issued by the Registrar's Office and include, at
minimum: student name, student ID, program, department, CGPA, credits
completed, per-semester GPA, and any failed courses. A transcript is
considered current if it was issued within the last 90 days.
